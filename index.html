How to order

No account needed.

1

Fill your cart

Add everything you like, then tap Cart and press Order on WhatsApp.

2

Send your order

Your order opens in WhatsApp, already written out. Press send and Saadatu confirms prices and delivery.

3

Pay by transfer

Payment is by bank transfer. We send the account details when your order is confirmed.

4

We deliver

Nationwide by waybill in 2 to 3 working days, for ₦4,500 to ₦6,000 depending on distance. We also ship worldwide.

All sales are final. We do not offer refunds.

Our story

Meet Saadatu

Being able to stand out and curate pieces with the same fingers everyone else has is the greatest blessing I could ever ask for. I started this brand with nothing but optimism. I had never crocheted any big piece before, but something deep inside assured me that I'd make the best out of just a hook and yarn.

I have crocheted and packed over 80 orders, and each one has taught me improvement and given me more determination to build my brand beyond my own drafts.

I am Saadatu, a Nigerian crocheter with over 2 years of experience, and I am proud to say I have learnt so much and refined my design complexity over the years.

Saadatu, The Yarn Yard
